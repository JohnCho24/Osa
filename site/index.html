The problem

Every match has fifty decisions you wish you could replay. Today, you can only argue about them.

Replays show what happened.

Broadcasters cut to a slow-motion angle. The play is the play. The alternative goes unwitnessed.

Tactics boards show what should have happened.

Pundits drag pieces around a static pitch. The board never plays out — the other twenty-one players are frozen.

Nobody can press play on the alternative.

Until now, the counterfactual was a thought experiment. We make it a video.

The product

One gesture from the coach. An entire alternative future from the model.

Coaches draw.

Click a player. Drag where you wish they had run. No keyboard, no JSON, no data science. The same gesture they already use on a touchscreen tactics board.

Our model predicts.

A diffusion model trained on multi-agent trajectory data generates the alternative future. Every other player moves plausibly. Max-speed, on-pitch and inter-player collision constraints are enforced. Classifier-free guidance lets the coach dial how strictly to honor their arrow.

3D video renders.

Trajectory output is shipped to a photorealistic AI video engine. The result is a broadcast-quality replay of the alternative play, indistinguishable from a real highlight reel — minus the ten million dollars in production crew.

How it works

Four steps from a moment in the match to a broadcast-ready alternative.

  1. 01

    Tracking ingest

    The league's existing optical-tracking feed (Hawk-Eye, Sportec DFL, Stats Perform, SkillCorner) is normalized into a common per-frame coordinate stream at 25 fps.

  2. 02

    Arrow input

    The analyst or operator opens the decision moment plus four seconds of preceding history. A click and a drag is the entire user interface.

  3. 03

    Diffusion + physics

    A waypoint-conditioned diffusion model samples a multi-agent alternative future. A deterministic physics post-processor enforces max-speed, on-pitch constraints and inter-player repulsion.

  4. 04

    Video handoff

    The alternative trajectory ships to a photorealistic AI video engine as a per-frame JSON. The broadcaster receives the cut as a normal video file.

The technology

Paper-faithful research, extended. Not a prompt wrapper.

Backbone

TimeSformer-style factorized spatiotemporal attention over the 23-entity trajectory tensor. d=256, four layers, eight heads.

Diffusion

DDPM with 100 reverse-diffusion steps, linear β-schedule, ε-prediction. Causal sliding-window rollout for long-horizon forecasting.

Extension

Learned waypoint conditioning with classifier-free guidance. The model sees per-entity arrow targets and trains under CFG dropout — coaches get a tunable "honor my arrow" knob.

Physics

Deterministic post-pass: seam anchoring, per-frame max-speed clip, exponential smoothing, on-pitch clamp with 2 m margin, pairwise inter-player repulsion.

Eval

ADE, FDE, inter-sample diversity, off-pitch violation rate and arrow-honoring error under a CFG sweep — reported per checkpoint, machine-readable.

Provenance

Built on GenTac (arXiv:2604.11786), TimeSformer (arXiv:2102.05095), DDPM (arXiv:2006.11239).
