The problem

Two tools. Neither one moves.

The replay

Six angles, frame-perfect — and stuck on the one decision that mattered. It can only ever show the play that was actually made. The other version doesn't exist on tape.

The tactics board

A pundit drags ONE arrow across a frozen still. The other 21 players never move. The pass never travels. It's a doodle pretending to be analysis.

GenTac →

Draw the same arrow — and everyone moves. The whole alternative plays out, plausibly, and renders to video. Not a description of the what-if. The what-if, on screen.

The how

Three steps. No data team.

If you can run a touchscreen tactics board, you can run this. No JSON, no notebook, no PhD.

01

Draw

Tap a player on a real moment from your league's own tracking. Drag the run you wish they'd made. Set how hard the model honours it.

1 arrow = the whole UI
02

Generate

A diffusion model rolls every player forward off your arrow. Teammates adjust, the defence reacts, the ball moves like a ball — sample a few, keep the best.

22 players + ball
03

Air it

A physics pass keeps it real, then it renders to broadcast quality. Straight to the studio feed, the app, or the review desk.

render-ready out

The where

Built to live inside a league — where the data, the broadcast and the clubs already sit together.
