Tech specs

Paper-faithful research. Not a prompt wrapper.

Backbone
Factorised spatiotemporal attention over the 23-entity trajectory tensor — spatial across players, temporal across time. d = 256, 4 layers, 8 heads.
Generative core
Conditional diffusion (DDPM), ε-prediction, ~100 reverse steps over the joint trajectory. Returns a distribution of futures, not one deterministic guess.
Control
Drawn arrow → per-entity waypoint, with classifier-free guidance. A weight tunes how strictly the arrow is honoured.
Constraints
Deterministic physics pass — seam anchoring, per-frame max-speed clip, exponential smoothing, on-pitch clamp (2 m margin), pairwise repulsion. 0.0% off-pitch.
Data in
Provider-agnostic ingest at 25 fps. Open Metrica live; Hawk-Eye, Sportec / DFL, Stats Perform, SkillCorner adapters scoped per engagement.
Output
Per-frame trajectories + a render-ready, broadcast-quality video file. Metrics reported per checkpoint against a constant-velocity baseline.
Provenance
Built on GenTac (arXiv:2604.11786), factorised attention (arXiv:2102.05095) and DDPM (arXiv:2006.11239).
