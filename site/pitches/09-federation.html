The institutional case

A point solution for one club. A product line for a league.

One club running this is interesting internally and invisible to everyone else. A competition running it compounds across every match, every season and every supporter — and only the competition holds all three inputs at once.

◆

You already own the inputs

The tracking is licensed to you, the broadcast is contracted to you, the clubs answer to you. The instrument that combines them should sit with you, not a vendor.

▣

It compounds, it doesn't fragment

One engine spanning broadcast, the app, the review desk and member clubs — instead of four vendors, four contracts and four data-export risks.

◈

A category only you can offer

The alternative, rendered for air, is footage no rival broadcaster or single club can produce. It strengthens the rights package itself.

Governance & integrity

Built to be trusted by the body that runs the game.

The alternative is a sensitive object — it touches contested decisions. So it ships with the controls an institution needs to stand behind it.

  • Constrained, not inventedOutputs are physics-bounded and trained against an honest constant-velocity baseline — plausibility you can defend, not freehand fabrication.
  • Repeatable & auditableThe same inputs and guidance settings reproduce the same study. Every review is logged for the technical committee.
  • Governed distributionYou decide what airs to the public, what reaches supporters, and what stays internal to the competition desk and clubs.
  • Data sovereigntyNo tracking and no outputs leave the league's environment for a third party. The IP in the alternative is yours.
