The session

The board can show what should've happened. It just can't show it happening.

Telestration freezes one frame and lets a pundit slide a single arrow across it.

It's a still photograph with a doodle on top. The other twenty-one players stay exactly where the camera left them. The pass you're describing never travels, the run never arrives, the defender never has to react.

So the most interesting question in football — what if he'd done the other thing? — has only ever had one honest answer: we'll never know.

we turn "we'll never know" into a clip you can watch.

GenTac takes the same gesture every coach already makes — point at a player, drag where they should have gone — and fills in everyone else. The teammates adjust. The defence collapses or doesn't. The ball travels at a speed a ball can actually travel. It's not a drawing of the alternative. It's the alternative, moving.
