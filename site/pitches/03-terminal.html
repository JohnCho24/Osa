// the run

What one query actually does.

No notebook, no export, no data team in the loop. A single drawn intention compiles into a multi-agent forecast and comes back as something you can put on screen.

01
Load the state. load --possession 67:14 --window 4s

The match's own optical tracking — Hawk-Eye, Sportec, Stats Perform, SkillCorner — normalised to a 23-entity tensor at 25fps.

02
Set the intent. set --player 8 --run into-channel --honour 0.7

One drawn arrow becomes a per-entity waypoint. Classifier-free guidance turns "honour 0.7" into how strictly the model obeys it.

03
Sample the future. diffuse --steps 100 --samples K

A conditional diffusion model rolls every player forward together. Draw K samples for a distribution of plausible alternatives, not one guess.

04
Constrain & return. clamp --speed --bounds --collisions | export

A deterministic physics pass keeps it real, then returns trajectories + metrics + a render-ready clip.
